A Pomodoro timer for focused work: choose how long to work, start it, and take a
break when it ends.

The countdown is driven by a deadline rather than by counting ticks, so it does
not drift, and an exact alarm means a finished session announces itself even if
Android has shut the app down in the meantime. Reopen it and the usual prompt is
waiting for you.

Features

* Work presets of 15, 30 and 45 minutes; break presets of 5, 10 and 15
* Any custom length from 1 to 180 minutes
* A depleting ring showing the time remaining, sized to your screen
* A completed-session count that survives the app being closed
* Three themes: Black, Paper and Mist
* A live countdown in the notification shade
* An optional "keep screen on" switch
* A layout that adapts to tall screens and to wide or short ones

Privacy

This app has no internet permission at all. It cannot send anything anywhere,
because it has no means of opening a network connection. There are no adverts,
no analytics, no accounts and no tracking of any kind. The only things it
remembers, your chosen theme and your session count, stay on your device.

Requires Android 8.0 or newer.

This is an early release: complete and usable, with further work planned.
