Fixes two bugs that could lose the end of a session.

A session could be counted and announced twice: cancelling an alarm could
overwrite the deadline identifying the session, letting a broadcast already in
flight slip past the duplicate check.

And a session finishing while the app sat in the background would beep and
vibrate but leave the notification shade empty, because the app withdrew the
notification it had just posted. Resuming after a reboot also left no alarm
armed until the app was reopened.

Backup is now disabled, matching the promise that nothing leaves your device.
